# PhishGuard Lean Business Canvas

Team: CyberSentinels. Builder: Edwin Mantsho. These business assumptions require validation; no partnerships or revenue are claimed.

## 1. Problem
Students receive convincing fake bursary notices, funding-fee requests and account-verification messages. They need understandable warning signs and a safe verification path. Existing alternatives include asking friends, searching manually and generic link scanners.

## 2. Customer segments
Primary users: South African university students, initially TUT students. Early adopters: students receiving funding, internship and campus account messages. Prospective buyers: university student-support and cyber-awareness departments; not confirmed customers.

## 3. Unique value proposition
A quick, explainable second opinion on suspicious messages, paired with student-specific examples and short practical learning activities.

## 4. Solution
Paste message or link; get a rule-based Low/Medium/High advisory rating, visible indicators and independent-verification advice. Optional Supabase cloud checking preserves the fallback flow. An interactive quiz teaches common warning signs. AI explanation code exists but is not active without provider configuration.

## 5. Channels
Student societies, orientation programmes, campus cybersecurity campaigns, university support desks and a shareable web app. These are proposed acquisition channels, not existing distribution agreements.

## 6. Revenue streams
Potential institution-paid awareness programmes, campus licences and training packages. Student self-service access remains free in the prototype. Validate willingness to pay before setting prices.

## 7. Cost structure
Frontend hosting, Supabase compute/database, optional AI inference, maintenance, domain, accessibility/security reviews and support. No cost or profitability claims are made.

## 8. Key metrics
Successful checks, clear understanding of findings, quiz completion, false positives/negatives on independently labelled examples, and verified student feedback. No analytics or message history are collected by the prototype; measurement would require a separate privacy decision.

## 9. Unfair advantage
Hypothesis: student-focused scenario knowledge and campus feedback could improve the learning experience. This is not yet a defensible moat. The current differentiation is audience focus and explainability.
